Audit headline

Complexity has not yet beaten memory.

The core ensemble’s trailing-52-week RMSE was $0.186. A persistence forecast on the identical dates achieved $0.112. The model is a research forecast, not evidence of production superiority.

Clock warning. The repository describes a Monday release, while EIA currently documents Tuesday publication of Monday-sampled retail prices. The report specifies the required time-contract correction.

Interpretation

The ensemble error was 1.67× persistence.

Ridge was the best learned component, but no reported learner beat the same-window last-value forecast. XGBoost was correctly rejected, and the LSTM sleeve was not evaluated because its upstream gate failed.
